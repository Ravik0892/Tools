## 📅 1. Optimal Launch Timing & Algorithm Rules

* **Launch Day & Time**: Product Hunt's daily leaderboard runs on **Pacific Standard Time (PST)**.
  * Leaderboard resets at: **12:01 AM PST** (**1:31 PM IST** in India).
  * Submitting between **12:05 AM PST and 2:00 AM PST** gives your product the full 22–24 hour window to accumulate momentum.
* **The #1 Golden Rule**: **Never say *"Please upvote me"* or share direct voting links.**
  * Product Hunt's anti-spam algorithm detects direct voting clusters and will remove upvotes or shadowban the post.
  * Instead, always say: *"I just launched OmniTool Hub on Product Hunt! I'd love your honest feedback and thoughts: [link to your post]"*.
