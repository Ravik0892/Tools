6 Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: What happens if I get an "Invalid Code" error?
Ensure you are entering the 12 numeric digits without any extra letters. For example, enter 428901234567 instead of UTR: 428901234567. Our smart system will automatically trim whitespace, but ensure the 12 digits match the number on your payment receipt.
Q: Can I use OmniTool Hub PRO on multiple devices or browsers?
Yes! Because OmniTool Hub is client-side, your PRO status is saved in your browser's local storage. If you switch to another laptop, desktop, or mobile browser, simply open the checkout modal on the new device, enter your same 12-digit UTR or License Key, and click "Unlock PRO Access" to restore PRO status immediately.
Q: What if I clear my browser history or cookies?
If you clear your browser's local storage, your PRO tier may reset to Free. Don't worry! You can simply re-enter your 12-digit UTR or Gumroad key anytime to re-activate your PRO access at no additional cost.
Q: Are my card or banking details stored on your server?
Never. OmniTool Hub does not operate backend servers that store financial information. All card transactions are processed securely through PCI-DSS certified gateways (Gumroad, Razorpay, PayPal). UPI payments go directly between your banking app and the payee bank via NPCI.
Q: Need manual assistance or refund inquiry?
If you encounter any difficulty with your payment or activation, please email us directly with your transaction screenshot at support@omnitoolhub.com. We respond to all developer inquiries within 24 hours.
