Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I extract specific capture groups instead of full matches?

Yes. You can toggle between Full Match, Group 1, Group 2, or custom template replacement (e.g. `$1: $2`).

Can it automatically remove duplicate matches?

Yes. The "Deduplicate Matches" toggle filters out repeated items so you get a clean unique list.

What flags are supported?

It supports all standard JavaScript RegExp flags: `g` (global), `i` (case-insensitive), `m` (multiline), and `s` (dotAll).

Can I export the extracted matches?

Yes. You can copy the list directly to your clipboard or download it as a plain text or CSV file.
