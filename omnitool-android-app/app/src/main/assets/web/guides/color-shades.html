Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between a tint and a shade?

A tint is created by adding pure white to increase lightness, while a shade is created by adding pure black to deepen darkness.

Can I export the generated scale directly into Tailwind CSS?

Yes. One click exports a ready-to-paste `tailwind.config.js` color object containing all keys from 50 to 950.

Does it support HSL or OKLCH color spaces?

Yes. The engine interpolates perceptual lightness curves to ensure smooth, natural color transitions without mudiness.

Can I copy individual HEX codes quickly?

Simply click any swatch in the scale to copy its HEX or RGB value to your clipboard.
