The Three Powerhouse Financial Engines

1. Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) & Wealth Accumulator Wealth Builder

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows investors to invest fixed sums regularly into index funds or mutual funds. By investing consistently through market highs and lows, you eliminate emotional market timing (Dollar/Rupee Cost Averaging).

  • Dual Mode (Monthly vs Target Goal): Choose whether to calculate the future corpus from a fixed monthly SIP, or enter a desired target wealth goal (e.g. $500,000 or ₹1 Crore) to compute the exact monthly investment required.
  • Step-Up SIP Accelerator: Toggle annual step-ups (e.g. increasing your monthly deposit by 5% or 10% each year as your salary increases). In years 15–30, step-ups can more than double your final maturity corpus.
  • Inflation Purchasing Power Checker: Computes the real purchasing power of your future corpus based on long-term CPI inflation rates.
SIP Formula: M = P × [ (1 + i)ⁿ - 1 ] / i × (1 + i)
2. Compound Interest & APY Calculator Exponential Growth

Calculates compound growth on initial lump-sum deposits combined with regular monthly or annual additions. Switch between Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual compounding frequencies to evaluate true Annual Percentage Yield (APY).

Compound Formula: A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ
3. Loan EMI & Mortgage Amortization Schedule Debt Elimination

Calculate exact monthly Equated Monthly Installments (EMI) for home loans, car loans, and student debt under the reducing balance method.

  • Prepayment Accelerator: Test both extra monthly principal payments and annual lump-sum prepayments (such as yearly work bonuses) to see how many years and interest dollars are shaved off your loan.
  • Yearly & Monthly Amortization Table: Toggle between high-level year-by-year amortization and detailed month-by-month repayment breakdowns.
  • 1-Click Copy Summary & CSV Export: Copy a formatted plan summary directly to your clipboard or download the complete amortization schedule to CSV.
EMI Formula: EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ] / [ (1 + r)ⁿ - 1 ]

Visual Stage: Donut & Growth Curves

Switch between the visual Donut Chart (to visualize the ratio of Principal Outflow versus Capital Gain or Interest Paid) and the Growth Curve Area Chart (to visualize the hockey-stick inflection point where accumulated returns surpass original contributions).
