Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the probability of a UUID v4 collision?

With 122 bits of true entropy, you would need to generate 1 billion UUIDs every second for roughly 85 years before having a 50% chance of a single duplicate.

Can I generate thousands of UUIDs at once?

Yes. Enter any quantity (from 1 to 5,000) to generate a clean list ready to copy or download as JSON/CSV/SQL insert values.

Can I format UUIDs without hyphens or in uppercase?

Yes. The configuration panel toggles Hyphens, Uppercase/Lowercase, and Braces `{}` formatting.

Does this generator require internet connectivity?

No. Modern browsers feature native `crypto.randomUUID()`, executing in local machine code with zero network overhead.
